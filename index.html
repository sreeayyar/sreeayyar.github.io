About

I am a PhD Candidate in Economics at the London School of Economics and Political Science, where I am supported by an ESRC PhD Studentship. My research spans development economics, environmental economics, and econometrics.

My primary work studies Ultra-Poor Graduation programs in Bangladesh, with a focus on spillover effects, general equilibrium dynamics, and the intersection of poverty and climate migration. I use randomised control trials, panel data, and administrative records.

Previously, I was a Research Assistant to Professor Eric French at University College London and an Occasional Research Assistant to Professor Gharad Bryan at LSE. I hold an MSc in Econometrics and Mathematical Economics (Distinction, ranked first) and a BA in Economics and Management (First Class Honours, ranked first) from the University of Oxford.
